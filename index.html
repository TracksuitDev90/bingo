The Blower For the caller. Blow the balls, put it on stream, and check winning cards. Run the game → Get a Card For players. A one-of-a-kind card, your pick of dauber, and a big BINGO! button. Deal me in →

How it works

  1. Caller opens the Blower and shares their screen.
  2. Players each grab a card. Every card is different.
  3. Daub numbers as they're called. Got a line? Hit BINGO! and send the link.
  4. Caller pastes the link into Check a card. The machine says if it's real.
