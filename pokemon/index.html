How it works

  1. Host shares the blower screen.
  2. Players get a card on the route shown on the sign.
  3. Daub Pokémon as they're called, then call bingo.

Every route has 75 Pokémon: 60 from Kanto and 15 from Johto. Each new game moves on to the next route, so they all get a turn.
